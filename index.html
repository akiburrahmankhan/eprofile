§ 01 About

A teaching practice
built on evidence.

Akibur Rahman Khan is an English language educator with a Master of Arts in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL) from the University of Dhaka, currently serving as Lecturer in the Department of English at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB).

His work combines classroom teaching with an active research and publication record in applied linguistics, ESL pedagogy, and educational technology, alongside sustained leadership in academic conference organization and TESOL community service across Bangladesh.
